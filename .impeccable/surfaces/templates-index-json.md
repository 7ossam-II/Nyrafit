# Homepage (Onyx night)

## Scope

The storefront homepage (`templates/index.json`) and the Nayra sections it uses, plus the theme-wide palette and fonts that Horizon's product, collection and cart pages inherit. Mode: Persuade.

## Audience, job, action

Young Moroccan women arriving on a phone from Instagram or TikTok, often at night in dark mode. Job: decide in minutes that this is the real Nayra and that ordering is safe. Action: open a product, pick a size, add to cart or order on WhatsApp.

## Proof and constraints

Only true claims: cash on delivery, WhatsApp confirmation before shipping, delivery by courier, NAYRA-branded pieces, a dedicated modest collection. No reviews, ratings, delivery times or sales numbers. Every photo, product, price and text comes from the Shopify admin; empty slots show branded placeholders. Owner pinned the look ("Onyx night") and the structure (patterns from a premium competitor's homepage, never its photos, text, colors or code).

## Direction contract

THESIS: The homepage is the logo turned into a night boutique: the logo's own black, its silver and blush N, its ring and sparkles. It refuses the cream editorial store (cream, burgundy, Playfair) and the neon gym (black, acid accent, condensed caps).

OWN-WORLD: Ground #121112 (the logo's black), raised onyx #1b191b, pearl text #f6f1f3, blush #e3a3b7 for actions, rose #e481a4 for sale and emphasis, silver #a2a0a1 hairlines. Bodoni Moda display with the hero's Darija word in blush italic; Jost UI, small caps-tracked labels. Logo ring as thin silver circles (icon rings, step rings, the outfit turntable), four-point sparkle glints, black satin under blush light. Pill actions, square-cut 4:5 photo frames, no hard shadows, no glass.

STORY: She recognises the logo's black, sees the drop at full height, learns cash on delivery and WhatsApp confirmation in one strip, picks a piece, orders in a few taps or on WhatsApp.

FIRST VIEWPORT: At 390×844: a 44px ticker (tall enough for its 44px pause button) with sparkle separators; header with centered 44px monogram, menu left, cart right; full-bleed hero (black satin until the owner's video or photo) filling the rest of the first screen, at least 33rem; Bodoni headline bottom-left at 44px over two lines, Darija word in blush italic; one French line; full-width blush pill "Voir la collection" with a WhatsApp outline pill under it, both inside the first screen in thumb reach.

FORM: Owner-pinned premium store structure (ticker, centered header, full-bleed hero, trust strip, tall product cards, collection tiles, signature outfit stage, modest feature, story, steps, Instagram strip, closing band, footer). Signature interaction: outfits turn in 3D on a silver ring floor as she swipes, the order ticket follows. Pinned by the owner over roll seed:5f9ae671b681699e.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance

## Memorable moment

The glowing N in the header over black satin, then the outfit stage turning on its silver ring.
