# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Young women in Morocco who train at the gym (sets, leggings, bodysuits) and follow Nayra on Instagram and TikTok. They arrive on their phones from a post, story or reel, often already knowing which piece they want, and decide in minutes whether to order. A second audience is hijabi women looking for modest activewear (burkinis and other covered pieces), served by a dedicated collection.

## Product Purpose

Nayra's Shopify store turns Instagram attention into cash-on-delivery orders without the owner confirming size, color, city and address by hand in direct messages. Success: shoppers pick the right size and color, order in a few taps, and the owner confirms by WhatsApp and ships.

## Positioning

A Moroccan gym brand with its own voice: playful, relatable "gym girl" humor in Darija, pieces branded NAYRA, and prices most Moroccan gym-goers can afford. It sits apart from imported international brands (price, local delivery, cash on delivery) and from anonymous local resellers (a real brand personality and its own branded pieces). The dedicated modest collection serves hijabi women most gym brands ignore.

## Operating Context

- Traffic comes almost entirely from Instagram and TikTok on phones; the link in bio and product links in captions or DMs are the entry points.
- Payment is cash on delivery. Shopify Payments is not available in Morocco; card payment may come later through a local provider.
- A local courier delivers and collects the cash. Orders are confirmed with the customer on WhatsApp before shipping.
- WhatsApp is also the channel for questions. The theme already has a floating WhatsApp button and a cash-on-delivery box with an "Order on WhatsApp" button in the cart.
- Currency: Moroccan dirham (MAD).
- The owner edits products, prices, photos and banners in the Shopify admin and theme editor; nothing product-related may be hard-coded.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Shopify theme built on Horizon 4.3.0 (Liquid, theme blocks, theme editor settings). Buildless; synced from GitHub `main`.
- Store language: French for navigation, product pages and checkout. Darija in Latin letters for headlines and personality only.
- Pricing is affordable (for example, leggings under 200 DH), so promotions, bundles and complete sets matter.
- Collections: gym sets, leggings, sports bras and tops, bodysuits, a modest collection (burkinis and covered pieces), and later gym accessories.
- Undecided: exact prices, size range, delivery fees and times, free-delivery threshold, return and exchange policy.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: Nayra (Instagram @nayra.fitwear). "NAYRA" is printed on leggings waistbands; the logo is an "N" monogram.
- Voice: playful, girly, humorous and relatable, mixing Darija in Latin letters with French and some English, as in the owner's captions ("Hadi hiya outfit li tstahla kola gymgirl", "Dress like a barby in the gym").

## Evidence on Hand

- Instagram @nayra.fitwear: about 4,300 followers and 230 posts (October 2026), with studio photos of real models on plain grey backgrounds, flat lays, and comic-style meme posts.
- Products seen on Instagram: powder-blue seamless set, mauve long-sleeve crop top with leggings, black and red one-piece shorts bodysuits, blue tie-dye set.
- No customer reviews, testimonials, ratings, press, delivery-time promises or sales figures exist on the site yet. Do not invent them.

## Product Principles

1. Phone first: every decision is judged on a phone, arriving from Instagram.
2. Order without chatting: clear sizes, colors and cash on delivery make WhatsApp a backup, not a requirement.
3. Trust before payment: show cash on delivery, delivery and returns plainly, using real photos and only true claims.
4. Same personality as Instagram: the site should feel like the account, not like a generic store.
5. Affordable, never cheap-looking: deals and bundles are easy to see, and the presentation stays polished.
